Ember Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

Ember is a personal YouTube client for Android TV, made by BurbsKlopek. This policy explains what Ember (formerly called Nook) does with your information.

Information Ember accesses

If you choose to sign in, Ember uses Google's TV sign-in to request read-only access to your YouTube account (the youtube.readonly permission). This is used only to show your subscriptions, playlists and library inside the app. Ember cannot post, comment, like, upload or change anything on your account.

Where your data is stored

Your sign-in token, settings, watch history, saved home feed and app statistics are stored only on your TV. Ember has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your personal information with anyone.

Third-party services

To work, Ember connects directly from your TV to: YouTube and Google (videos, sign-in and the YouTube Data API), SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app) to skip sponsored segments, Return YouTube Dislike (returnyoutubedislike.com) to show dislike counts, and, only if you turn it on, DeArrow (dearrow.ajay.app) for crowd-sourced titles and thumbnails. Those requests include the ID of the video you are watching (DeArrow title lookups send only a short hash prefix of it). These services have their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Google API data

Ember's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Sign out in Ember's settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall Ember to delete everything it stored. You can also revoke Ember's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Ember is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
